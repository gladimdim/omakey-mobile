# Omakey 1.2.1 for Android

*Released October 8, 2026* · [Download omakey-1.2.1.apk](https://github.com/gladimdim/omakey-mobile/releases/download/v1.2.1/omakey-1.2.1.apk) · [GitHub release](https://github.com/gladimdim/omakey-mobile/releases/tag/v1.2.1)

## ✨ What's new

### 🖱️ Pointer speed on top
The pointer speed slider and its preset chip now sit at the top of the touchpad, in both landscape and portrait mode. The bottom edge is left to the grab bar.

## 💡 Good to know
- Swipe up from the grab bar along the bottom, or from a side button, to put the touchpad away, as before.
- Works with the Omarchy plugin 1.2.0, or 1.1.0 without the shared clipboard.

## ⬆️ How to update
Install the APK over your current version — your pairings are kept. ✅
