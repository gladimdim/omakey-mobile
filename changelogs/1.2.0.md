# Omakey 1.2.0 for Android 🎉

*Released October 8, 2026* · [Download omakey-1.2.0.apk](https://github.com/gladimdim/omakey-mobile/releases/download/v1.2.0/omakey-1.2.0.apk) · [GitHub release](https://github.com/gladimdim/omakey-mobile/releases/tag/v1.2.0)

## ✨ What's new

### 📋 Copy and Paste with your computer
- ✂️ **Copy** copies what's selected on the computer and puts it on your phone's clipboard too.
- 📥 **Paste** pastes on the computer — and brings your phone's clipboard along when you copied something newer on the phone.
- 🔁 Copy on the phone, paste on the desktop, and the other way round.
- 🔒 Passwords stay marked as sensitive. The app never stores your copied text.
- 📏 Up to 64 KB of text.

### 📍 Where to find them
- 📱 **Portrait mode:** Copy and Paste icons in the top bar.
- ⌨️ **Omakey Pro:** Copy and Paste keys inside the U-shaped Enter.
- 🛠️ **Your own layouts:** add `KEY_COPY` and `KEY_PASTE` keys in the [layout studio](https://gladimdim.github.io/omakey-layout-studio/).

### 🧹 Cleaner top bar
Icons only — no more text labels.

### 🎮 Type on a Steam Deck
A Steam Deck with SteamOS is now a computer you can type on, no Omarchy needed. See the [plugin changelog](https://github.com/gladimdim/omakey-omarchy-plugin/blob/main/changelogs/1.2.0.md).

## 💡 Good to know
- The shared clipboard needs the Omarchy plugin **1.2.0** (or the Steam Deck install). Everything else works with 1.1.0.
- In Bluetooth keyboard mode, with an older computer setup, or in Steam Deck's Game Mode, Copy and Paste simply press the copy and paste keys.
- On Android 12+, a "pasted from your clipboard" notice appears when Paste reads your phone's clipboard. That's normal.

## ⬆️ How to update
Install the APK over your current version — your pairings are kept. ✅
