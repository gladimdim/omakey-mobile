## ✨ What's new

### 🌙 Wake on LAN
Open a computer that's asleep, and your phone wakes it.

- 💤 A computer that doesn't answer but can be woken shows **Asleep or off · opening it wakes it** on the main screen.
- 📱 Tap it. If it doesn't answer within 2 seconds, the phone sends it a Wake-on-LAN packet, and a few more while it wakes up. The status says **Waking…**, and the keyboard connects as soon as the computer is up.
- ⚙️ Turn it on on the computer first: **Settings → Wake on LAN** in the Omarchy bar widget, or `omakeyd wake-on-lan on`. The phone learns it the next time it connects.
- 🏠 It works from the same Wi-Fi, even with a VPN like Tailscale on.
- 📶 Over Wi-Fi a computer wakes from sleep, not from power-off. Over Ethernet it can wake from power-off too.
- 🔕 Don't want it? **Settings → Wake on LAN** on the phone turns it off. Settings also shows which computers can be woken.

### 📶 Wi-Fi only, for now
Bluetooth is off in this release: no Bluetooth keyboard mode and no Bluetooth fallback to the computer, so the app no longer asks for the Nearby devices permission. Omakey works over Wi-Fi as before.

## 💡 Good to know
- Wake on LAN needs the Omarchy plugin **1.4.0** on the computer. Everything else works with 1.2.0 or newer, or 1.1.0 without the shared clipboard.
