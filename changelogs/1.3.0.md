# Omakey 1.3.0 for Android

*Released October 9, 2026* · [Download omakey-1.3.0.apk](https://github.com/gladimdim/omakey-mobile/releases/download/v1.3.0/omakey-1.3.0.apk) · [GitHub release](https://github.com/gladimdim/omakey-mobile/releases/tag/v1.3.0)

## ✨ What's new

### ✏️ Your own key row in portrait mode
The keys above your phone's keyboard are now two rows:
- ⭐ **The upper row is yours.** Ten slots that stay put, for the keys you reach for most. Empty slots show as dashed outlines.
- ↔️ **The lower row swipes** through digits, F1–F12, navigation, and Super, Ctrl, Alt, Print Screen and media keys, as before.

To arrange the upper row, tap the ✏️ pencil at its right end. The keys start trembling, like icons on an iPhone's home screen:
- ⬆️ Drag a key up from the lower row into a slot, or tap it to fill the first empty slot.
- 🔀 Drag a key along the upper row to move it. A key already in that slot swaps places with it.
- ✖️ Tap a key in the upper row, or drag it down off the row, to clear its slot.
- ✅ Tap the tick when you're done. Your row is kept for next time.

Outside editing, every key in both rows works as before: tap to type, hold to repeat, and hold or tap Super, Ctrl, Alt and Shift for shortcuts.

### 🔵 Bluetooth icon
The status pill, the computer picker, the main screen and Settings show the Bluetooth sign when the phone is typing over Bluetooth: as a Bluetooth keyboard, or when omakeyd falls back from Wi-Fi.

## 💡 Good to know
- The upper row starts empty. Fill it once and it stays.
- Works with the Omarchy plugin 1.2.0 or newer, or 1.1.0 without the shared clipboard.
